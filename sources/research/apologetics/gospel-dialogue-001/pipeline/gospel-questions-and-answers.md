# Gospel Questions And Answers

## Why did Jesus have to die?

Because sin is not small. If God is holy and just, sin must be dealt with. Jesus died to deal with guilt, not just to set an example.

## Why can’t God just forgive?

God does forgive, but He does not ignore justice. If a judge simply waves away guilt, he is not righteous. The cross is where God’s justice and mercy meet.

## Why is blood necessary?

Blood means life given. In Scripture, it points to the cost of sin and the seriousness of forgiveness.

## Why is death the payment for sin?

Because sin separates us from God, and separation from the source of life ends in death. The Bible uses death language to show how serious rebellion is.

## Why can good works not cancel sin?

Good works are good, but they do not erase guilt. A clean record is not the same as a forgiven record. We need mercy, not just effort.

## What does repentance mean?

Repentance means turning around. It means turning from sin and turning toward God.

## What does faith mean?

Faith means trusting Jesus. It is not pretending. It is leaning your whole weight on Him rather than on your own works.

## Why is Jesus different from other religious teachers?

Jesus does not merely point to truth. He claims to be the truth, and His death and resurrection are central to that claim.

## Why is Christianity different from other religions?

Christianity says God saves sinners by grace through Christ. It does not say we climb up to God by our own merit.

## What about ayahuasca or other spiritual experiences?

Spiritual experiences can feel powerful, but experience by itself does not tell you what is true. You still need a standard to test what you are encountering.

## Who made God?

God is not one more thing inside the universe. He is the Creator. Created things need a maker; the Creator is not treated as a created thing.

## Why did God allow the serpent?

Because real love and real obedience require real freedom. God did not make robots. He made persons who can choose.

## Why is there evil?

The Bible says evil entered through sin. That does not make evil small; it makes evil explainable without making it good.

## Which Bible should I read?

Read a clear translation that you can actually understand. The Gospel of John or Mark is a good place to start.

## What should I do if I feel drawn to Jesus?

Do not ignore that. Read a Gospel, pray honestly, ask God to show you the truth, and be willing to repent and trust Jesus if He is making Himself clear to you.
