# Source Summary V3

## What I Extracted From `01-proof-for-jesus-miracles-and-supernatural.md`

### Strongest historical case material

- Jesus existed as a historical figure.
- Jesus was crucified under Pontius Pilate.
- The crucifixion was a public Roman execution, which makes the resurrection claim historically significant.
- The tomb is presented as empty.
- Women are listed as first witnesses, which is treated as historically awkward if the story were invented.
- The disciples changed from fearful hiding to public proclamation.
- James and Paul are presented as skeptic-to-believer conversions.
- 1 Corinthians 15:3-8 is treated as an early creed.
- The resurrection is presented as the best explanation of the data.
- Miracles are presented as possible if God exists.
- Naturalism is treated as the deeper worldview issue.

### Strongest resurrection material

- The resurrection is not presented as a standalone proof but as a cumulative historical case.
- The case uses multiple lines: crucifixion, empty tomb, appearances, transformed disciples, converted skeptics, and early creed.
- The argument is that resurrection best explains the full data set.

### Strongest miracle/supernatural material

- If God exists, miracles are possible.
- A closed naturalism assumption is what blocks miracles before the evidence is weighed.
- The real debate is worldview-level, not only historical detail.

### Phrases and analogies worth preserving

- “None of these alone force belief. Together? They form a cumulative case.”
- “If God exists, resurrection is possible.”
- “The resurrection hypothesis explains all of it in one move.”
- “That’s either the greatest delusion in history. Or the pivot point of reality.”

### Material to filter out

- Overstated claims like “all scholars agree.”
- Overconfident prophecy language that needs careful support.
- Casual references like “Google these figures.”
- Claims that sound like propaganda instead of careful apologetics.
- Excessive insistence that skeptics already have their mind made up.

### Claims needing source support

- Tacitus, Josephus, and Pliny references.
- Exact dating of the 1 Corinthians 15 creed.
- “Most scholars” style claims.
- Strong prophecy claims.
- Claims about group appearances and hostile authorities.

## What I Extracted From `02-the-gospel-legal-framework.md`

### Strongest gospel/legal material

- God is holy and righteous.
- God is Creator and Judge.
- Sin is lawbreaking.
- The wages of sin is death.
- God cannot ignore sin and remain righteous.
- Jesus died in the sinner’s place.
- Jesus paid with His blood.
- Jesus rose because death had no rightful claim on Him.
- The resurrection is God’s vindication of Jesus.
- Repentance and faith are the response.
- Salvation is grace, not earned works.

### Strongest legal/courtroom structure

- Judge
- law
- courtroom
- accuser
- guilt
- payment
- substitute
- advocate
- debt
- vindication

- The debt/payment language.

- Overly harsh claims about other religions.
