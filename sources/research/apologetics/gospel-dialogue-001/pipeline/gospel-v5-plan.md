# Gospel v5 Plan

## What Changed from v4

v4 was structurally correct but had two problems:
1. Too long — nineteen sections of dense legal argument.
2. Language level too high — assumes the reader knows Bible terminology, has familiarity with theological concepts, and can hold a long analytical argument.

v5 targets a 16-year-old with no Bible background. Short sentences. Plain language. Every quote immediately translated into plain English. No unexplained terms.

## Three Core Points That Must Be Crystal Clear in v5

These three concepts drive the entire revision. Every structural decision serves them.

### 1. Why is God good if He allowed killing and suffering?
Answer: Because when the final moment came, God paid the price Himself. He did not send someone else. He entered His own creation as a human being and absorbed the full sentence in His own body. The cross is the definitive answer to the accusation that God is cruel. No cruel God pays His own debt for people who turned against Him.

### 2. Where does your morality come from?
Answer: Every person appeals to a standard they did not invent. The moment you call something *truly* wrong — not just personally disliked, but actually wrong — you are appealing to a law above you. That law is God's. He built it into every human being. Every religion is ultimately about morality. Every religion except one says you can earn your way back through works. Christianity alone says: the debt is already there, someone else paid it. That makes Christianity uniquely debt-based, not work-based. This is the most important distinction between Christianity and every other worldview.

### 3. Why is blood the currency? What does the cross actually do?
Answer: Life is in the blood. Blood equals life in both the physical and spiritual world. Because sin earns death (the loss of life), the only payment that cancels the debt is life — blood. This is a universal principle: every occult and pagan religion throughout history has demanded blood, especially innocent blood (children, because innocence carries more weight in the spiritual realm). They are operating on the same principle, but inverting it — they demand your sacrifice, or the sacrifice of others, to pay your debt. The gospel says God paid it Himself, in the most innocent blood possible: Jesus, who lived a sinless life as both man and God, whose innocence meant death had no legal claim on Him, which is precisely why He could rise from the dead. The resurrection is not just a miracle — it is the legal proof that the payment was accepted and the debt cleared.

## Five-Stage Pipeline for v5

### Stage 1 — Case Construction
Source: `gospel-v4-plan.md`, `02-the-gospel-legal-framework.md`
Output: the legal case structure (compressed into 8 sections max)

Purpose: Identify the minimum set of logical steps the reader must take. v4 had 19 sections. v5 targets 8. Cut everything that doesn't directly advance one of the three core points above.

Sections kept:
1. Morality — where does your standard come from?
2. God as Creator — why He has authority
3. The Fall — what went wrong and why it matters for morality
4. The legal problem — sin as a debt, not just a mistake
5. Blood as currency — plus the occult/pagan confirmation of this principle
6. The cross — God's answer: fully human, fully divine, sinless, took the debt
7. The resurrection — legal proof the payment was accepted
8. The response — repentance and faith, what they actually mean

### Stage 2 — Draft at 16-Year-Old Level
Source: Stage 1 structure + three core points
Output: full letter draft at simplified reading level

Rules:
- Short sentences. If a sentence has three clauses, break it into three sentences.
- Every Bible quote is immediately followed by "Plain English: ..." that restates it in simple terms.
- No theological term is used without immediate definition in plain language.
- No assumption that the reader has ever read the Bible or attended church.
- KJV quotes are used for accuracy but never left unexplained.

### Stage 3 — Three Core Points Check
Output: verification that each of the three core points is clearly present and clearly stated

For each point, check:
- Is it present?
- Is it stated in plain language a 16-year-old can understand?
- Is the answer clear, not just the question?

### Stage 4 — Length Check
Output: word count check. v5 target is 1,500–2,000 words (v4 was ~4,000+). If over 2,000 words, identify and cut the lowest-value paragraphs.

### Stage 5 — Simplification Pass (NEW)
This is the final gate before the letter is published.

Check every paragraph against these rules:
- **No assumed vocabulary**: any word a 16-year-old might not know gets replaced or immediately defined
- **No assumed Bible knowledge**: every reference to a Bible story is briefly explained even if referenced earlier
- **No complex sentence structures**: passive voice, subordinate clauses, and long parenthetical asides are rewritten as short active sentences
- **KJV quotes**: every KJV quote is followed by "Plain English: ..." immediately below it
- **Analogies**: are they from everyday modern life? Cut analogies from legal practice, ancient culture, or academic philosophy unless they are translated into terms a teenager actually uses
- **Tone**: remove any remaining "document-tone" or "essay-tone" phrasing. Write like you are explaining something to a sharp teenager at a table, not submitting a paper

After this pass, the letter should be readable aloud without anyone needing to stop and ask "what does that mean?"

## What Is Removed from v5 vs v4

- Standalone section on other worldviews (Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism, New Age) — compressed to one short paragraph within the morality section
- Extended courtroom role-play (Judge, Accuser, Defendant sections) — kept as short plain-language overview only
- Long "Tree of Life" mercy-in-judgment section — removed
- Detailed prophecy / Bible reliability section — removed (referenced once briefly)
- Long closing "compare your beliefs" paragraph — replaced with direct personal call

## What Is Added in v5

- Morality question as the opening hook (where does your standard come from?)
- Explicit statement: every religion is about morality; only Christianity is debt-based not work-based
- Blood / occult / pagan connection (the three core points requirement)
- "Plain English:" translation line under every KJV quote
- Simplification pass as final pipeline stage
