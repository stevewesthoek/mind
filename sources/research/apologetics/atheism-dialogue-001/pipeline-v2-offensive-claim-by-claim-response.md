**Challenge back:**

**Direct response:**

**Correction:**  
Steve is not denying science; he is denying the leap from methodological naturalism to metaphysical naturalism.

**Support:**  
Philosophy; SEP naturalism; SEP naturalized epistemology.

**Challenge back:**  
Show that metaphysical naturalism is proven, not merely useful.
