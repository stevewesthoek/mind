## C001 — Faith vs. Trust

**Their claim:**

**Classification:**  
logical fallacy / philosophical claim / accusation

**Direct response:**

I meant that atheism and naturalism rely on starting commitments that are not themselves established by science. That is a different claim. You are right that ordinary trust based on repeated observation is not the same thing as blind faith. But you are wrong to act as if that distinction eliminates the philosophical question of what justifies those starting commitments.

**What I concede, if anything:**  
My wording was too broad if read as collapsing all trust into religious faith. That needs tightening.

**What I reject:**  
I reject the conclusion that this makes the argument irrelevant. It does not.

**Why the reasoning fails or needs support:**  
The response attacks a flattened definition of faith, not the actual epistemic point. This is a false equivalence complaint against a weaker version of my claim.

**Corrected formulation:**  
Faith is not the same as every kind of trust, but worldview-level commitments still need justification, and atheism/naturalism does not get to treat those commitments as self-explanatory.

**Support / source needed:**  
philosophy
