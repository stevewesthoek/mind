# Historical Jesus Notes

## Scope

This folder should hold only historical claims relevant to the resurrection case, not devotional conclusions.

## Dialogue Claim

Steve argues that the historical facts about Jesus, including the empty tomb and rapid rise of Christianity, are best explained by the resurrection.

## What Needs Support

- Jesus existed.
- Jesus was crucified.
- The early disciples believed they saw the risen Jesus.
- Early Christian proclamation is historically early.
- Alternative explanations exist and must be compared carefully.

## What Should Not Be Overstated

- The resurrection is not established by "history alone" in a neutral way.
- Rapid growth of Christianity does not by itself prove resurrection.
- Later texts and oral traditions require careful handling.

## Research Questions

- What are the earliest independent sources?
- Which facts are accepted by a broad range of historians?
- What are the strongest naturalistic explanations?
- What historical inference rules are being used?
- Are we arguing for best explanation or for certainty?

## Best Framing

- Treat the resurrection as a cumulative historical inference.
- Compare it with naturalistic alternatives.
- Distinguish historical minimal facts from theological interpretation.
