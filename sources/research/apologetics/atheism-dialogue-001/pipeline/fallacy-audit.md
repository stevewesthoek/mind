# Fallacy Audit

## Standards Used

- Steelman both sides first.
- Separate logical defects from mere disagreement.
- Mark major claims as supported, plausible but unsourced, disputed, weak, or remove.
- Keep Scripture, philosophy, science, and history distinct.

## Formal Fallacy Risks

- False equivalence: faith vs warranted trust.
- False dilemma: materialism versus God as exhaustive options.
- Equivocation: "atheism" as lack of belief vs positive disbelief.
- Equivocation: "information" in DNA vs semantic information.
- Equivocation: "science" as method vs worldview.
- Burden-shifting: demanding atheists prove nonexistence.
- Category error: using abiogenesis objections against evolution.
- Category error: using science to settle metaphysical claims without argument.
- Straw man: treating all atheism as scientism.
- Overclaim: treating unresolved science as proof of God.

## Claims To Remove From A Sendable Final Response

- Transitional fossils are absent.
- Entropy rules out evolution.
- Breeding only removes information.
- Science tells us the universe came from nothing.
- Atheism is a religion because it answers big questions.
- All atheists are committed to scientism or naturalism.
- Consciousness has not been shown to arise from physical processes at all.
