# Apologetics Case File: baptism-dialogue-001

## Purpose

This case file explores the theological dialogue around water baptism, grace, predestination, and assurance of salvation. It contains structured biblical questions and responses examining whether baptism is a requirement for salvation, whether believers can lose their salvation, and how God's sovereignty relates to human agency.

The dialogue involves two voices:
1. **Questioner** — Someone emphasizing biblical assurance and God's sovereign preservation of believers
2. **Respondent** — Someone with a different conviction on baptism's role and warnings about falling away
