## Unresolved Tensions

| Area | Position 1 | Position 2 | Resolution |
|------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Nature of warnings | Exam tools | Threat warnings | Context suggests exam (1 Cor 13:5) |
| Baptism's role | Sign of membership | Means of remission | Acts 10:44-48 separates them chronologically |
| What "falling away" means | Revelation of non-election | Loss of election | 1 John 2:19 resolves in favor of revelation |
| OT applicability | Typological only | Typological + literal | NT directly teaches typological fulfillment |

---

## Strong Consensus Points

1. **God is sovereign.** Both agree. Question is scope.
2. **Christ's blood is the actual payment.** Both fully agree.
3. **Baptism is important.** Both agree. Question is whether it's sign or cause.
4. **Wilson is saved.** Both agree. This is not academic for them.
5. **Romans 14 principle applies.** Both agree disagreement shouldn't fracture fellowship.

---

## Weakest Links

**Position 1:**
- How to explain Hebrews' repeated warnings without acknowledging loss as possible?
  - Answer: Warnings are for self-examination, not warning of actual loss.

**Position 2:**
- How can someone be "really justified" and then "fall away" if justification is God's unilateral act?
  - Answer: Tends to assume warnings = possible outcomes, but 1 John 2:19 reframes this.

---

## Doctrinal Classifications

| Doctrine | Position 1 | Position 2 |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Justification | Forensic, immediate, irrevocable | Forensic, might be conditional on persistence |
| God's Sovereignty | Complete; foreknowledge ensures outcomes | Complete; but permits warnings with real consequences |
| Election | Unconditional (Ephesians 1:4-5) | Conditional on belief + perseverance |
| Assurance | Warranted by God's character | Warranted but contingent on obedience |
| Water Baptism | Ordinance/sign of faith | Sacrament/means of grace |

---

## Summary

**Position 1 is internally coherent** if:
- Apostasy proves non-election (1 John 2:19)
- Warnings function as sanctification tools, not threat warnings
- Baptism's role is sign, not cause

**Position 2 is internally inconsistent** if:
- God can foreordain all things (Romans 9) but someone still "falls away"
- Baptism is necessary but chronologically unnecessary (Acts 10:44-48 shows Spirit before baptism)
- Warnings mean "you might lose it" but Ephesians 1:13-14 says you're sealed
