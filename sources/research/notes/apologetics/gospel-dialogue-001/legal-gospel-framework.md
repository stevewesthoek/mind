# Legal Gospel Framework

## Core Analogy

The gospel can be explained through a courtroom frame:

- God is Creator
- God is holy
- God is righteous Judge
- sin is lawbreaking against God
- the wages of sin is death
- humans cannot pay for sin with good works
- Jesus lived sinlessly
- Jesus died as a substitute
- Jesus paid the debt
- resurrection shows death had no rightful claim on Him
- repentance and faith are the response
- salvation is grace, not earned works

## Simple Explanation

A judge who is good does not ignore evil. If a law is broken, justice matters. If someone guilty is simply waved through without any reckoning, that is not justice. The gospel says God is both just and merciful: He does not pretend sin does not matter, and He provides what guilty people cannot provide for themselves.

## Terms to Explain Clearly

- **Holy:** morally pure and set apart from evil
- **Sin:** rebellion against God and breaking His law
- **Righteousness:** being right with God and right in what God calls good
- **Repentance:** turning from sin to God
- **Grace:** undeserved favor
- **Judgment:** God’s just evaluation of sin
- **Atonement:** the payment or covering for sin

## Guardrails

- Do not make God sound mechanical or cruel.
- Keep justice and love together.
- Keep blood, payment, and debt language only where it is biblically clear and easy to understand.
- Use concrete examples, not long abstractions.
