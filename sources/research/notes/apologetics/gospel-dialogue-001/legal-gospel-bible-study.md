# The Gospel Through God’s Courtroom

## 1. God owns the world because He created it

**Bible quotation:**
- Genesis 1:1: “In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.”
- Psalm 24:1: “The earth is the LORD’s, and the fulness thereof.”
- John 1:3: “All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.”

**Plain explanation:**
God is not part of creation. He made creation, owns creation, and has authority over it.

**Legal meaning:**
The Creator has rightful authority over what He made.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
If God made us, then our lives are not our own. We are answerable to Him.

## 2. God is holy and righteous

**Bible quotation:**
- Leviticus 19:2: “I the LORD your God am holy.”
- Isaiah 6:3: “Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of hosts.”
- Revelation 4:8: “Holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty.”

**Plain explanation:**
God is morally pure. He is not mixed with evil.

**Legal meaning:**
The Judge is not corrupt.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
A holy God cannot pretend evil does not matter.

## 3. God is Judge

**Bible quotation:**
- Genesis 18:25: “Shall not the Judge of all the earth do right?”
- Psalm 96:13: “he cometh to judge the earth.”
- Hebrews 9:27: “after this the judgment.”

**Plain explanation:**
God judges rightly and will judge every person.

**Legal meaning:**
There is a real court and a real verdict.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
The gospel is not about escaping reality; it is about standing before the righteous Judge with a real answer.

## 4. God has given law

**Bible quotation:**
- Exodus 20:1: “And God spake all these words.”
- Matthew 22:37-40: “Thou shalt love the Lord thy God ... Thou shalt love thy neighbour.”
- Romans 3:19-20: “that every mouth may be stopped.”

**Plain explanation:**
God gives commands that define right and wrong.

**Legal meaning:**
Law tells us what is required and what is forbidden.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
If God gives law, then guilt is possible when the law is broken.

## 5. Sin is lawbreaking

**Bible quotation:**
- 1 John 3:4: “sin is the transgression of the law.”
- Romans 3:23: “For all have sinned.”
- Isaiah 59:2: “your iniquities have separated between you and your God.”

**Plain explanation:**
Sin is not just a mistake. It is rebellion against God.

**Legal meaning:**
Sin creates guilt and separation.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
If sin is real, then we need more than self-improvement.

## 6. The wages of sin is death

**Bible quotation:**
- Genesis 2:17: “thou shalt surely die.”
- Romans 6:23: “For the wages of sin is death.”
- James 1:15: “sin ... bringeth forth death.”

**Plain explanation:**
Sin earns a real penalty.

**Legal meaning:**
The sentence matches the offense.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
Death is not random here; it is connected to sin’s guilt.

## 7. Blood represents life given for atonement

**Bible quotation:**
- Leviticus 17:11: “it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul.”
- Hebrews 9:22: “without shedding of blood is no remission.”
- Matthew 26:28: “my blood ... shed for many for the remission of sins.”

**Plain explanation:**
Blood means life given. Atonement means covering or payment for sin.

**Legal meaning:**
The debt is answered by a costly payment.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
The cross is not emotional symbolism only. It is payment language.

## 8. Satan is the accuser

**Bible quotation:**
- Job 1:9-11: Satan appears before God and accuses Job.
- Zechariah 3:1: “Satan standing at his right hand to resist him.”
- Revelation 12:10: “the accuser of our brethren.”

**Plain explanation:**
Scripture pictures Satan as the accuser who presses guilt.

**Legal meaning:**
There is a prosecution, not only a defense.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
The sinner needs more than self-defense; he needs an advocate.

## 9. Jesus is Advocate and Mediator

**Bible quotation:**
- 1 John 2:1: “we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous.”
- 1 Timothy 2:5-6: “one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus.”
- Hebrews 7:25: “to make intercession for them.”

**Plain explanation:**
Jesus speaks for sinners and stands between us and God as mediator.

**Legal meaning:**
Jesus is the defense and the bridge.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
We do not save ourselves; Jesus represents us.

## 10. Jesus is the substitute who bears sin

**Bible quotation:**
- Isaiah 53:5-6: “the LORD hath laid on him the iniquity of us all.”
- 2 Corinthians 5:21: “he hath made him to be sin for us.”
- 1 Peter 2:24: “Who his own self bare our sins in his own body on the tree.”

**Plain explanation:**
Jesus takes the sinner’s place.

**Legal meaning:**
The substitute bears what the guilty deserved.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
The gospel is not God relaxing justice. It is God providing a substitute.

## 11. God justifies sinners through Christ

**Bible quotation:**
- Romans 3:24-26: “being justified freely by his grace ... that he might be just, and the justifier.”
- Romans 5:1: “being justified by faith, we have peace with God.”
- Romans 4:5: “his faith is counted for righteousness.”

**Plain explanation:**
God declares sinners righteous through Christ.

**Legal meaning:**
The court declares a verdict based on Christ, not self-effort.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
God remains just and still saves the guilty.

## 12. Redemption means the debt is paid

**Bible quotation:**
- Mark 10:45: “to give his life a ransom for many.”
- Colossians 1:13-14: “redemption through his blood.”
- Titus 2:14: “who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us.”

**Plain explanation:**
Redemption means release because the price has been paid.

**Legal meaning:**
The debt is satisfied.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
Jesus did not merely advise sinners; He paid for their release.

## 13. Salvation is by grace, not works

**Bible quotation:**
- Ephesians 2:8-9: “by grace are ye saved through faith ... not of works.”
- Titus 3:5: “Not by works of righteousness which we have done.”
- Romans 11:6: “if by grace, then is it no more of works.”

**Plain explanation:**
We do not earn salvation.

**Legal meaning:**
The guilty are not declared righteous because they improved themselves.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
Grace means God saves by mercy, not merit.

## 14. The resurrection shows Jesus is vindicated

**Bible quotation:**
- Romans 4:25: “raised again for our justification.”
- Acts 2:24: “it was not possible that he should be holden of it.”
- 1 Corinthians 15:3-8: “he was buried, and ... he rose again.”

**Plain explanation:**
Jesus really rose from the dead.

**Legal meaning:**
God’s public verdict vindicates Jesus.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
The resurrection shows the substitute is accepted and alive.

## 15. The right response is repentance and faith

**Bible quotation:**
- Mark 1:15: “repent ye, and believe the gospel.”
- Acts 16:31: “Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved.”
- Romans 10:9: “if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus.”

**Plain explanation:**
Repentance means turning from sin toward God. Faith means trusting Jesus.

**Legal meaning:**
The guilty person does not self-justify; he turns to the only sufficient advocate.

**How this helps explain the gospel:**
The Bible’s answer is not try harder; it is trust Christ.
