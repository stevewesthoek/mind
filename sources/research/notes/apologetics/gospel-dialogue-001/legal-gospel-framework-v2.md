# Legal Gospel Framework

## 1. Moral law

People make moral judgments every day. We say things are right, wrong, fair, cruel, unjust, or good. That only makes sense if there is a real standard behind the judgment.

If there is no objective standard, then moral judgment collapses into preference. If there is a real standard outside us, then morality points beyond us.

## 2. Lawgiver and authority

Law requires authority. Authority requires a rightful judge. God is not judged by the law; God is the source of the law. God’s law flows from His character.

That means the moral standard is not arbitrary. God is holy, which means He is morally pure and set apart from evil. He is righteous, which means He always does what is right.

## 3. Sin as lawbreaking

Sin is not only a mistake. Sin is guilt before God. Sin is rebellion against rightful authority. Sin brings judgment because breaking real law has real consequences.

## 4. Courtroom picture

The courtroom picture helps make the gospel plain:

- God is Judge
- Satan is the accuser
- conscience bears witness
- the law exposes guilt
- every person stands accountable
- no one can bribe the court with good works

## 5. Why God cannot simply ignore sin

A judge who ignores evil is not good. If God ignored sin, He would not be righteous. Justice requires a real answer to guilt.

That is why Scripture uses death, judgment, and payment language. The language is sober because sin is serious.

## 6. The wages of sin is death

Wages means payment. Sin earns death. Death is not random in this framework; it is judgment.

That should be explained soberly, not harshly. The point is not to be theatrical. The point is to be truthful.

## 7. Jesus as substitute and advocate

Jesus lived without sin. He had no guilt of His own. That means He could stand in the place of sinners.

Jesus died as a substitute. He paid the debt sinners could not pay. He is not merely a teacher; He is Savior, Substitute, Advocate, and Lord.

## 8. Blood and payment

Blood means life given. The price of sin is life. Jesus gave His life willingly.

That is why blood language appears in the gospel. It is not a magic word. It is justice and love meeting at the cross.

## 9. Resurrection as vindication

Death had no rightful claim on sinless Jesus. The resurrection shows Jesus was vindicated. The resurrection confirms the payment was accepted.

The resurrection means Jesus is alive, not merely remembered.

## 10. Repentance and faith

Repentance means turning from sin toward God. Faith means trusting Jesus, not your own works.

Salvation is not earned. Grace means undeserved mercy. The right response is to come to Jesus honestly.
