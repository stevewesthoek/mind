# External Sources

## Philosophy And Worldview

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Atheism and Agnosticism

- Author / institution: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy
- URL: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/atheism-agnosticism/
- Supports: atheism has multiple meanings; atheism is not identical to a full worldview
- Reliability note: High; standard reference
- Caution / limitation: Philosophical overview, not a neutral verdict on theological truth

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Naturalism

- Author / institution: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy
- URL: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/naturalism/
- Supports: methodological naturalism vs metaphysical naturalism; naturalism as a broad philosophical position
- Reliability note: High; standard reference
- Caution / limitation: Terminology is contested across fields

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Philosophy of Religion

- Author / institution: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy
- URL: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/philosophy-religion/
- Supports: worldview questions, consciousness, cosmos, religion, and secular naturalism as legitimate subjects of philosophical analysis
- Reliability note: High; standard reference
- Caution / limitation: Broad survey rather than a specific argument

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Naturalism in Epistemology

- Author / institution: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy
- URL: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epistemology-naturalized/
- Supports: the idea that epistemology and science are related, but not identical; useful for science-as-method framing
- Reliability note: High
- Caution / limitation: Does not settle metaphysical naturalism

## Moral Realism

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Moral Naturalism

- Author / institution: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy
- URL: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/naturalism-moral/
- Supports: moral realism, naturalistic moral realism, and the fact that moral questions remain philosophically live
- Reliability note: High
- Caution / limitation: Complex and technical; does not prove a theistic moral argument

## Historical Jesus / Resurrection

### The Resurrection of the Son of God

- Author: N. T. Wright
- URL / reference: HarperOne, 2003
- Supports: resurrection as a historical claim requiring broad explanatory comparison
- Reliability note: Widely respected scholarly work
- Caution / limitation: Large and detailed; final response should use it as background, not dump its entire argument

### The Case for the Resurrection of Jesus

- Author: Gary R. Habermas and Michael R. Licona
- URL / reference: Kregel, 2004
- Supports: cumulative historical case and minimal-facts style framing
- Reliability note: Useful apologetics source
- Caution / limitation: Apologetic orientation should be acknowledged

### Why Are There Differences in the Gospels?

- Author: Michael R. Licona
- URL / reference: Oxford University Press, 2016
- Supports: careful historical reading of Gospel differences and resurrection narratives
- Reliability note: Strong scholarly treatment
- Caution / limitation: Not a direct resurrection proof
