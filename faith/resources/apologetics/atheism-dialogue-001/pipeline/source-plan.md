# Source Plan

## Purpose

This plan identifies which claims can stay in the final response and what kind of source support each needs.

## Keep, But Source Carefully

| Claim area | Use in final? | Source need | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Atheism vs naturalism vs scientism | yes | philosophy source needed | Use SEP definitions and keep the distinctions explicit. |
| Faith vs warranted trust vs justified belief | yes | philosophy source needed | Define faith carefully so it does not collapse into "anything not proven." |
| Science as method vs scientism as philosophy | yes | philosophy source needed | Distinguish methodological naturalism from metaphysical naturalism. |
| Methodological naturalism vs metaphysical naturalism | yes | philosophy source needed | This is central to the response. |
| Consciousness and the hard problem | yes | philosophy source needed; science source helpful | Say consciousness remains philosophically underexplained rather than denying brain/mental correlation. |
| Moral realism / moral argument | yes | philosophy source needed | Keep as an inference, not a proof. |
| Fine-tuning as an inference, not proof | yes | philosophy source needed; science source helpful | Use cautious language and acknowledge alternatives. |
| Historical Jesus / resurrection as cumulative historical argument | yes | historical source needed | Present as inference to best explanation. |
| Biblical posture for apologetics | yes | Bible source needed | Use passages for tone and framing, not as shortcuts. |

## Claims That Should Stay Out Unless Better Sourced

| Claim area | Use in final? | Source need | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transitional fossils are absent | no | remove | Already rejected by evidence review. |
| Entropy makes evolution impossible | no | remove | Already rejected by evidence review. |
| Breeding only removes information | no | remove | Already rejected by evidence review. |
| Science says the universe came from nothing | no | remove | Overclaim and likely false as stated. |
| Atheism is a religion in the same sense as Christianity | no | remove | Rhetorically useful but definitionally unstable. |
| All atheists are committed to scientism | no | remove | Overgeneralization. |
| Consciousness has not been shown to arise from physical processes at all | no | remove | Too strong and too easy to refute. |

## Recommended Final-Response Sources By Section

### 1. Definitions and worldview framing

- SEP on atheism and agnosticism
- SEP on naturalism
- SEP on philosophy of religion

### 2. Science and philosophy

- SEP on naturalism in epistemology
- SEP on evolutionary epistemology or cognition and reliability
- A source on methodological naturalism versus metaphysical naturalism

### 3. Consciousness

- A philosophy of mind source on the hard problem
- A neuroscience source showing brain/mental correlation

### 4. Morality

- SEP on moral naturalism / moral realism
- A source on objective moral realism or moral arguments for God

### 5. Fine-tuning

- A philosophy of religion source on the fine-tuning argument
- A cosmology source if a universe-beginning claim is retained

### 6. Resurrection

- A historical Jesus / resurrection source that treats the argument as cumulative

### 7. Biblical framing

- 1 Peter 3:15-16
- 2 Timothy 2:24-26
- Romans 1:18-25
- Psalm 19:1-4
- Acts 17:22-31
- 1 Corinthians 15:3-8
