# Philosophy Notes

## Core Distinctions

### Atheism

- In the dialogue, Steve often uses atheism as a positive metaphysical worldview.
- The respondent uses atheism as lack of belief.
- These are not interchangeable.

### Faith

- "Faith" should not be flattened into "belief without evidence."
- Better distinctions:
  - blind faith
  - warranted trust
  - justified belief
  - revealed faith

### Science

- Science is a method.
- Scientism is a philosophical claim about the limits of science.
- Methodological naturalism is not the same as metaphysical naturalism.

### Logic and Mathematics

- Logic and mathematics are not physical objects.
- That does not by itself prove theism.
- A theistic argument must still explain why abstract order, rationality, and applicability matter.

### Morality

- Moral realism is not identical to theism.
- Moral anti-realism is not required by atheism.
- The moral argument works only if Steve argues from objective moral facts, not merely social disapproval.

### Consciousness

- Correlation with brain states is strong evidence that consciousness is embodied.
- Correlation does not settle ontology.
- A better claim is that consciousness remains philosophically and scientifically underexplained.

### Epistemology

- The evolutionary argument against naturalism is a real philosophical argument.
- It should be stated as a defeater argument, not as a knockdown proof.
- Overstating it invites easy rebuttal.

## Fallacy Checks

- Do not make unresolved questions into proof of God.
- Do not make lack of certainty into irrationality.
- Do not convert worldview critique into personal accusation.
- Do not use "science" and "naturalism" as synonyms.

## Best Philosophical Revision

The strongest philosophical line is not "atheists are faith-based too."

It is:

- atheism alone is too thin to answer the questions the dialogue is really asking
- naturalism must defend its own metaphysical and epistemic assumptions
- theism may offer a more integrated account of reason, morality, and meaning
