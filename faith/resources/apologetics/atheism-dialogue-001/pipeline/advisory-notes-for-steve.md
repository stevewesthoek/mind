# Advisory Notes For Steve

## Purpose

These are caution notes backed by source evidence. They do not weaken the public position; they identify where wording should stay disciplined.

## 1. Bare atheism is not identical to naturalism

Evidence:

- SEP `Atheism and Agnosticism` distinguishes definitions of atheism and notes the relation to metaphysical naturalism.
- SEP `Naturalism` distinguishes methodological naturalism from philosophical naturalism.

Advisory:

- Keep saying the stronger target is atheism joined to naturalism or scientism.
- Keep the functional religion argument explicit: atheism is not merely a legal category; it functions as worldview-level allegiance and false worship redirected away from God.
- Do not back down to the opponent’s narrow institutional-only definition of religion; that narrow definition is not the controlling definition for Steve’s argument.

## 2. Science does not settle its own metaphysical presuppositions

Evidence:

- SEP `Naturalism in Epistemology` says naturalized epistemology is a philosophical position closely connected to science, not a mere lab result.
- SEP `Naturalism` explicitly distinguishes methodological naturalism from philosophical naturalism.

Advisory:

- Keep the line that science presupposes reason, logic, induction, and uniformity.
- Do not frame this as "science is broken"; frame it as a philosophical grounding issue.
- Do not let the note be read as if Steve is conceding that naturalism wins the right to define reality.

## 3. Consciousness remains a live problem

Evidence:

- PMC article on the hard question of consciousness.
- PMC article on the hard problem historically reconstructed.

Advisory:

- Keep the phrase that consciousness is embodied and brain-linked.
- Keep the phrase that correlation does not equal full explanation.
- Do not claim the brain explanation is absent; claim the ontology remains open.

## 4. Origin of life remains unresolved

Evidence:

- PMC origin-of-life review says the problem remains one of the most challenging scientific questions.

Advisory:

- Keep the claim that abiogenesis is unresolved.
- Do not say chemistry is irrelevant.
- Do not say the origin of life is solved by amino acids, synthetic biology, or current lab work.

## 5. Fine-tuning is an inference, not a proof

Evidence:

- SEP `Fine-Tuning` treats the issue as a real philosophical problem, not a scientific proof.

Advisory:

- Keep the design inference language.
- Do not call it proof.
- Do not let the anthropic principle be presented as if it fully explains the data.

## 6. Resurrection should stay cumulative and historical

Evidence:

- Cambridge sources and Britannica support the historicity of Jesus, the crucifixion context, and the historical debate around resurrection claims.

Advisory:

- Keep the phrase "cumulative historical case."
- Do not let the letter sound as if one datum alone proves resurrection.
- Do not concede the "2,000-year-old hearsay" framing.
- Keep the historical case framed as source-quality, proximity, explanatory scope, and comparison, not as a soft appeal to tradition.

## 7. Bible usage should remain posture-first

Evidence:

- `1 Peter 3:15-16` uses defense/apologia language with gentleness and respect.
- `2 Timothy 2:24-26` explicitly warns against quarrelsome speech.
- `Romans 1:18-25`, `Psalm 19:1-4`, `Acts 17:22-31`, and `1 Corinthians 15:3-8` support the framework when used contextually.

Advisory:

- Use Scripture for posture and theological framing, not as a shortcut.
- Do not proof-text or sound preachy.
- Do not soften the rebuttal just because the verses call for gentleness.

## 8. What To Keep Sharp In Public Wording

- "That is not what I argued."
- "That conclusion does not follow."
- "This is an assertion, not an argument."
- "That is a category error."
- "That misrepresents my position."
- "That is a motive accusation, not an argument."

## 9. What To Avoid

- "Atheism is just another religion" as a strict institutional/legal equivalence claim.
- "Science proves materialism."
- "Consciousness is solved by neuroscience."
- "Origin of life is solved."
- "Fine-tuning proves design."
- "Resurrection is uncontested."

## 10. Functional Religion Framing

Evidence:

- Romans 1:18-25 redirects truth suppression into creature-worship.
- Psalm 14:1 frames denial of God as moral folly, not neutrality.
- Colossians 1:15-20 grounds ultimate allegiance in Christ’s supremacy.
- 2 Corinthians 10:5 frames apologetics as dismantling rival thoughts and allegiances.

Advisory:

- Present atheism as a functional religious allegiance problem, not as a mere denomination or institution problem.
- Ask the opponent to justify why the functional definition is invalid.
- Do not let them retreat to "I’m not in a religion, therefore I’m neutral."
