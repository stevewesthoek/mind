# Resurrection Legal Meaning Expanded

## Death as the wage of sin

Paul says, “For the wages of sin is death” (Romans 6:23). Death is not random in the moral sense. It is tied to guilt.

## Sinlessness of Jesus

Hebrews says Jesus was “yet without sin” (Hebrews 4:15). He did not carry His own guilt.

## Death’s lack of rightful claim

If Jesus had no sin, death had no rightful legal claim on Him. Acts says, “it was not possible that he should be holden of it” (Acts 2:24).

## Resurrection as legal vindication

Resurrection is the public verdict of heaven. It shows Jesus is righteous and that His mission was accepted.

## Resurrection as accepted payment

Romans says Jesus “was raised again for our justification” (Romans 4:25). The resurrection confirms the cross was not a failure.

## Resurrection as ongoing mediation

The risen Christ is still active for His people. “Who also maketh intercession for us” (Romans 8:34). “He ever liveth to make intercession for them” (Hebrews 7:25).

## Resurrection as basis for judgment and hope

Acts says God “hath given assurance unto all men, in that he hath raised him from the dead” (Acts 17:31). First Corinthians says if Christ is not raised, faith is vain (1 Corinthians 15:17). First Peter says the resurrection brings “a lively hope” (1 Peter 1:3).
