Build a full Bible scan of legal, courtroom, covenant, and redemption themes to support the gospel framework with tighter source coverage.

## 14. Future scan method

1. Search each category through the whole Bible.
2. Group passages by legal function, not just by word match.
3. Record whether each passage is primary, secondary, or support only.
4. Note whether the passage is direct legal language, sacrificial language, or courtroom imagery.
5. Separate doctrinal texts from narrative illustrations.
6. Keep a list of passages that are strong enough to quote directly in a follow-up letter.
