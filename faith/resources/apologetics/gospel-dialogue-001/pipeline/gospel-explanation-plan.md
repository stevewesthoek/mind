# Gospel Explanation Plan

## Planned Structure

1. Personal opening
2. Why this matters
3. Who God is
4. What sin is
5. Why judgment is just
6. Why works cannot save
7. What Jesus did
8. Why the resurrection matters
9. What repentance and faith mean
10. What response is needed
11. Invitation to talk, pray, and read Scripture together

## Guidance

- Keep the structure simple and direct.
- Use the legal/courtroom frame as a way to clarify the gospel.
- Avoid church jargon unless it is explained.
- Keep the letter personal and first person.
