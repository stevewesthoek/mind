## The Idea in 30 Seconds

**Tiny Word Play** is a parent-led, screen-minimal first-words learning system for babies 6–24 months. It uses the Look-Find-Share methodology (joint attention + real objects + responsive interaction) to teach babies their first words while keeping screen time minimal.

**Core Hook:** "The baby word system that helps your child look away from the screen."

---

## Why It's Different

- **NOT competing with Cocomelon** (passive entertainment)
- **IS competing with Montessori/Waldorf/RIE resources** (parent-led learning)
- **White space market:** Zero dominant competitors in parent-led, screen-minimal space
- **Addressable market:** 2–3 million English-speaking parents

---

## The Science: Solid ✓

- ✅ Look-Find-Share is evidence-based (AAP-aligned)
- ✅ Parent-led learning outperforms passive screen
- ✅ Real-object manipulation enhances word encoding
- ✅ Research backs every claim (not pseudoscience)

## The Business Model

**Revenue Streams:**
1. **Printable products** (PRIMARY) — €9–€19 Starter Pack
2. **YouTube ads** (secondary) — grows with channel
3. **Subscription community** (scaling) — €8–10/month
4. **App** (future) — freemium model
5. **Multilingual packs** (expansion) — Portuguese, Dutch, Spanish

**Printables are the engine:** High margin (70–90%), not dependent on algorithm.
