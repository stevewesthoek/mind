## THE METHOD: Look–Find–Share

**Three core steps:**

1. **Look**
   - Baby sees one clear object
   - Principle: Joint attention
   - Science: Foundational for word learning

2. **Find**
   - Parent finds and manipulates the real-world object
   - Principle: Sensorimotor experience
   - Science: Hands-on interaction enhances word encoding

3. **Share**
   - Parent names, points, waits, responds, repeats
   - Principle: Responsive interaction
   - Science: Timing and contingency drive vocabulary growth

## YOUTUBE CHANNEL FORMAT

### Video Specifications

**Length:** 2–4 minutes

**Structure:**
- 0:00–0:10 — Parent instruction
- 0:10–0:45 — Word 1 (show, name, pause for response)
- 0:45–1:20 — Word 2 (same format)
- 1:20–1:55 — Word 3 (same format)
- 1:55–2:15 — Repeat all three words
- 2:15–2:30 — Call-to-action (download printables)

**Example Script (Kitchen Words):**
- "Sit with your baby. Hold the screen yourself. Today we'll practice three kitchen words together."
- "Cup. This is a cup. Can you show your baby a real cup? Pause and wait. Any look, sound, reach, or smile counts."
- (Repeat for Spoon, Milk)

### Visual Style (Faceless AI Content)

**What to Include:**
- Soft neutral background
- One realistic object at a time
- Large simple word overlay
- Warm human-like voiceover
- Gentle parent instruction
- High contrast, recognizable by toddler

**What to AVOID:**
- Fantasy details
- Fake cartoon eyes
- Over-saturated nursery style
- Moving baby characters
- Uncanny children
- AI baby teacher avatar

**Production Quality Rules:**
1. Every object must be recognizable by a toddler
2. No surreal AI errors
3. No extra objects in frame
4. No text embedded inside generated images
5. Word text added in video layer, not generated into image
6. Same background style across all episodes
7. Manual review of every image before publishing
