# Router Taxonomy

## Note types

- `capture` — raw or lightly processed intake.
- `task` — actionable work item.
- `project` — outcome with a finish line.
- `decision` — committed choice that should guide future work.
- `source` — raw evidence, transcript, article, file, or reference.
- `wiki` — compiled durable knowledge.
- `workflow` — repeatable operating process.
- `archive` — completed, inactive, or superseded material.

## Routing hints

- High-action items should update `live/tasks.md` or `live/projects.md`.
- Durable knowledge should compile into `wiki/`.
- Raw evidence belongs in `sources/`.
- Temporary or unclear captures remain in `capture/inbox/` until reviewed.
- Errors and failed saves belong in `capture/failed/`.
