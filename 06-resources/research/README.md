# Research

This is the research home inside the Mind repo.

Use this area for exploratory, source-grounded work: notes, source files, briefs, reports, and bibliographies that are still being gathered, compared, or tested.

Research is distinct from committed strategy. When findings become a settled decision, move or synthesize them into `02-strategy/`. When the work becomes executable, use `04-tasks/`. When it is an active delivery effort, use `03-projects/`.

Use Git as the canonical history for this vault. Use Google Drive as an import/export/collaboration layer when source material arrives from or needs to go to Drive, but keep the repo as the durable home.

Before writing new research content, AI should read `AGENTS.md` and `00-memory-map.md`, then search only the relevant subfolder.

## Layout

- `inbox/` - raw captures and unsorted research intake
- `sources/` - imported source material and evidence files
- `notes/` - working research notes by topic
- `briefs/` - short synthesis documents
- `reports/` - longer synthesis or findings reports
- `bibliographies/` - source lists and references
- `templates/` - reusable research note templates
- `metadata/` - lightweight mapping and ledger files
