# Legal Gospel Climax Notes

## Build-up

1. Start with earthly court logic.
2. Define the legal roles clearly.
3. Show that morality already implies standard, authority, and judgment.
4. Show that the Bible identifies God as Judge.
5. Show that sin is lawbreaking, not just bad feeling.
6. Show that guilt is real and good works do not erase it.
7. Show that the wages of sin is death.
8. Show that blood represents life given and payment for sin.
9. Show that Satan accuses and that Job gives a glimpse of heavenly courtroom imagery.
10. Show that Jesus is Advocate, Mediator, and Substitute.
11. Bring the explanation to the cross as the climax.
12. Show resurrection as vindication and accepted payment.
13. End with repentance and faith as the right response.

## What the climax must communicate

- we are guilty before God
- Satan accuses
- God is a righteous Judge
- God cannot ignore sin
- there is a price to be paid
- the price is life and blood
- Jesus steps in as Mediator and Advocate
- Jesus pays the price with His own life
- Jesus did this for people who did not deserve it
- this is the greatest declaration of love the world has ever known
- if we accept Christ, we are washed by His blood and clothed in His righteousness
- when God looks at those who belong to Christ, they are justified in Him, not self-cleansed by works
- this is why Jesus is not optional
- this is why Christianity is unique
- this is why the cross is the center

## Voice instruction

This is not a devotional or homiletic message.

This is not a vague story.

This is factual, biblical, legal, and logical explanation.

The reader may not know the gospel.

Assume the reader thinks they are good because they compare themselves with others.

Show that the Bible’s standard is God’s holiness.

Bring home the realization that Jesus paid His life for guilty people who did not deserve it.

## Delivery notes

- Use the legal framework as the bridge of understanding.
- Keep the structure readable and serious.
- Weave Scripture into the explanation instead of listing only references.
- Let the cross feel like the point everything has been moving toward.
- Keep resurrection as vindication, not a separate topic.
