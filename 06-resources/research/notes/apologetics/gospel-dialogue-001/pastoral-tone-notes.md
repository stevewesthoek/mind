# Pastoral Tone Notes

## Tone For This Person

- not aggressive
- not manipulative
- not debate-first
- not trying to win an argument
- clear call to respond
- honest about sin and judgment
- gentle enough for someone seeking Jesus
- direct enough to avoid hiding the cost of following Christ

## Voice

- first person
- warm
- clear
- serious
- truthful
- biblical
- not churchy unless terms are explained

## Practical Guidance

- Invite questions.
- Explain before pressing.
- Keep the gospel central.
- Do not minimize repentance, faith, or the cross.
