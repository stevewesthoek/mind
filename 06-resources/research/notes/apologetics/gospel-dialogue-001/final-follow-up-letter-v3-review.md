# Final Follow-Up Letter V3 Review

## Strengths

- The legal/courtroom frame is now the spine of the letter.
- Jesus is central as substitute, savior, advocate, and risen Lord.
- The historical case is included without overwhelming the gospel explanation.
- The tone is warm, direct, and personal.
- The letter answers the person’s likely questions without turning into a sermon outline.

## Places That May Be Too Long

- The opening explanation of law and authority could be shortened slightly if needed.
- The historical paragraph may be a little dense for a first follow-up letter.

## Places That May Be Too Blunt

- “He is the Lord you must answer to” is strong, but appropriate.
- “I am asking you not to hide” is direct; it works if the surrounding tone stays warm.

- Yes.

- Yes.
- The letter makes the death and resurrection of Jesus the turning point.

## Whether It Uses the Raw Source Docs Properly
