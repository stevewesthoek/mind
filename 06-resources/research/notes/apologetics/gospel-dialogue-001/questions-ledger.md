# Questions Ledger

## Likely Questions

1. Who is God?
2. Why did Jesus have to die?
3. Why blood?
4. Why can’t God just forgive?
5. Why is sin so serious?
6. Why are other religions false?
7. What about ayahuasca or other spiritual experiences?
8. Who made God?
9. Why did God allow the serpent?
10. Why is there evil?
11. Which Bible or translation should I use?
12. What must I do to be saved?

## Notes

- These questions should be answered directly, not dodged.
- The final letter should explain terms instead of assuming church vocabulary.
- The response should be honest about sin and judgment without becoming harsh.
