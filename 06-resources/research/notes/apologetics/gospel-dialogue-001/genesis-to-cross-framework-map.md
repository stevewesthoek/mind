- creation
- garden
- command
- tree of life
- tree of knowledge
- serpent
- fall
- death
- law
- court
- accuser
- substitute
- cross
- resurrection
- response

## Framework notes

### Creation

God made the world good. The story begins with ownership, design, and communion.

### Garden

Eden is the place of fellowship, command, and moral order.

### Command

God gives a clear boundary before sin. The legal framework begins here.

### Tree of life

Access to ongoing life is real, and later blocked after sin.

### Tree of knowledge

The temptation is moral autonomy: defining good and evil apart from God.

### Serpent

The serpent tempts, deceives, and introduces rebellion.

### Fall

Disobedience brings shame, blame, separation, and death.

### Death

Death is the wage and consequence of sin.

### Law

God’s command defines guilt.

### Court

The legal frame explains judgment, guilt, and justice.

### Accuser

Satan presses the charge against guilty people.

### Substitute

Jesus enters the guilty place and bears the penalty.

### Cross

The cross is the payment point where justice and love meet.

### Resurrection

The resurrection is the verdict that the payment was accepted.

### Response

Repentance and faith are the right answer to the gospel.
