# Pipeline V2 V3 Source Appendix

## Purpose

This appendix sits next to the v3 sendable response and gives Steve source backup for the most disputed claims. It is concise by design.

## 1. Atheism As Functional Religion / Worldview / Redirected Allegiance

- **Claim:** atheism joined to naturalism/materialism/scientism can function as a religion/worldview/false worship system.
- **Plain-language explanation:** a worldview is not just a private opinion; it answers big questions about what exists, what matters, and who or what gets ultimate trust.
- **Logic check:** the premise is not “atheism is always a church.” The premise is “a worldview that answers ultimate questions can function religiously.” The conclusion is that a functional definition is reasonable even if the institutional definition is narrower.
- **Existing support:** `source-and-proof-pack.md`, `bible-support-map.md`, `advisory-notes-for-steve.md`, `pipeline-v2-evidence-ledger.md`
- **Recommended sources to retrieve:** SEP entries on atheism and naturalism; a dictionary or encyclopedia definition of worldview; a secular source on “ultimate concern” or worldview religion language
- **Public-use wording:** “The issue is whether this package functions as a worldview and a form of redirected allegiance.”
- **Internal caution:** do not let the public response collapse into a narrow legal or institutional definition of religion.

## 2. Science And Metaphysics

- **Claim:** science is a method and does not by itself prove metaphysical naturalism.
- **Plain-language explanation:** science tells us how to test things; it does not tell us whether only matter exists.
- **Logic check:** method → worldview is a disputed inference, not a proven step.
- **Existing support:** `source-and-proof-pack.md`, `pipeline-v2-evidence-ledger.md`
- **Recommended sources to retrieve:** SEP `Naturalism`; SEP `Naturalism in Epistemology`; philosophy of science references from a university or encyclopedia source
- **Public-use wording:** “That explains the method, not the worldview behind the method.”
- **Internal caution:** keep this philosophical, not anti-science.

## 3. Logic, Math, And Reason

- **Claim:** describing logic and mathematics as “descriptive tools” does not by itself ground their universal validity.
- **Plain-language explanation:** saying “it works” is not the same as explaining why it should bind everyone.
- **Logic check:** description ≠ grounding.
- **Existing support:** `source-and-proof-pack.md`
- **Recommended sources to retrieve:** philosophy of mathematics; logic realism vs nominalism; law of logic references
- **Public-use wording:** “You have described them, but you have not grounded them.”
- **Internal caution:** explain hard terms like normativity in plain language if used.

## 4. Induction And Reason

- **Claim:** observation is not the same thing as justifying induction.
- **Plain-language explanation:** repeated success in the past does not logically guarantee the future will be the same.
- **Logic check:** observed regularity does not equal guaranteed future regularity.
- **Existing support:** `source-and-proof-pack.md`
- **Recommended sources to retrieve:** Hume / problem of induction sources; an accessible philosophy explainer on induction
- **Public-use wording:** “Science uses induction. Science does not prove induction.”
- **Internal caution:** keep the point concise so it does not become a lecture.

## 5. Consciousness

- **Claim:** brain dependence does not prove materialism; correlation is not ontology.
- **Plain-language explanation:** seeing a relationship between brain and mind does not tell you what mind ultimately is.
- **Logic check:** correlation/dependence ≠ full explanation.
- **Existing support:** `scientific-source-map.md`, `pipeline-v2-evidence-ledger.md`
- **Recommended sources to retrieve:** hard problem of consciousness sources; philosophy of mind references; neuroscience caution on correlation and causation
- **Public-use wording:** “Brain dependence is real, but it does not settle what consciousness is.”
- **Internal caution:** avoid sounding like brain science is being dismissed.

- **Plain-language explanation:** knowing why people cooperate is not the same as knowing why people ought to do what is right.
- **Logic check:** description of behavior does not equal moral authority.
- **Existing support:** `source-and-proof-pack.md`, `pipeline-v2-evidence-ledger.md`
- **Recommended sources to retrieve:** moral realism / anti-realism sources; evolutionary morality sources; metaethics references

## 8. Fine-Tuning

- **Claim:** the anthropic principle does not by itself explain why there is a life-permitting universe.
- **Plain-language explanation:** it tells you why observers notice a suitable universe, not why the universe is suitable.
- **Logic check:** observational selection is not a causal explanation.
- **Existing support:** `scientific-source-map.md`, `pipeline-v2-evidence-ledger.md`
- **Recommended sources to retrieve:** SEP `Fine-Tuning`; a concise cosmology or philosophy-of-science source
- **Public-use wording:** “The anthropic principle explains observation, not causation.”
- **Internal caution:** keep fine-tuning framed as inference, not proof.

- **Logic check:** historical claims use source quality and comparative reasoning, not lab replication.
