## Remaining Source Risks

- `science does not itself prove` is a philosophy claim, not a lab claim.
- `the historical data often discussed in resurrection studies` should not imply uncontested consensus.
- `meaning` and `moral obligation` are better treated as philosophical questions than as settled empirical facts.

## Appendix Candidates

- A compact note on why science depends on non-scientific assumptions.
- A compact note on why consciousness remains philosophically underexplained.
- A compact note on why the resurrection is argued as a cumulative historical case.
- A compact note on why fine-tuning is an inference rather than a proof.
