# Bible and Theology Notes

## Tone Guardrails

Use these as posture controls, not proof-texts:

- 1 Peter 3:15-16: defend with gentleness, respect, and a clear conscience.
- 2 Timothy 2:24-26: avoid quarrelsome speech; aim for patient correction.

## Scripture That Is Genuinely Relevant

### General Apologetics Posture

- 1 Peter 3:15-16
- 2 Timothy 2:24-26

### Reason, Order, and Creation

- Romans 1:19-20 - creation reveals enough to render humanity without excuse.
- Psalm 19:1-4 - creation declares God's glory.
- Isaiah 1:18 - the invitation to reason together.

### Wisdom and Knowledge

- Proverbs 1:7 - the beginning of knowledge is the fear of the Lord.
- Colossians 2:3 - in Christ are hidden all treasures of wisdom and knowledge.

### Resurrection / Historical Claim

- 1 Corinthians 15:3-8 - early witness summary to the resurrection claim.

## What Scripture Does Not Do Here

- Scripture does not settle modern cosmology by itself.
- Scripture does not replace historical investigation.
- Scripture does not justify overclaiming scientific certainty.
- Scripture does not support caricaturing atheists as merely rebellious or stupid.

## Theology Notes

- The dialogue should not claim that every atheist consciously denies God in the same way.
- The stronger theological claim is that humans suppress truth and need patient, truthful witness.
- The argument should remain cumulative: creation, conscience, rationality, and resurrection together.
- Avoid claiming that unresolved science equals proof of God.

## Relevant Theological Distinctions

- Methodological naturalism versus metaphysical naturalism
- Lack of belief versus positive denial
- General revelation versus special revelation
- Public evidence versus private testimony
- Apologetic argument versus salvific proclamation
