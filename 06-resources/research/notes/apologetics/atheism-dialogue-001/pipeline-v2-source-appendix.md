## 2. Atheism As Functional Religion / Worldview

- **Functional definition being defended:** atheism can function as a worldview and a redirected allegiance system even when it is not a formal institution.
- **How this differs from institutional/legal religion:** institutional/legal religion asks whether something is a recognized church-like body; Steve’s claim is functional and worldview-based.
- **Already available source notes:** `source-and-proof-pack.md`, `advisory-notes-for-steve.md`, `bible-support-map.md`
- **Recommended source to retrieve:** SEP entries on atheism and naturalism, especially for careful definitional language
- **How to use in public response:** state the functional claim plainly and ask the opponent to prove why that definition is invalid rather than merely narrow the category

## 3. Science, Naturalism, And Metaphysics

- **Core distinction:** methodological naturalism vs metaphysical naturalism
- **Why science does not prove metaphysical naturalism:** science is a method; metaphysical naturalism is a philosophical conclusion
- **Already available source notes:** `source-and-proof-pack.md`, `pipeline-v2-evidence-ledger.md`
- **Recommended source to retrieve:** SEP `Naturalism`, SEP `Naturalism in Epistemology`
- **Public-response use:** keep the claim philosophical, not anti-science

## 4. Logic, Induction, And Reason

- **Issue:** induction and rational intelligibility are presupposed, not proven by the act of science itself
- **Already available source notes:** `source-and-proof-pack.md`
- **Recommended source to retrieve:** SEP `Naturalism in Epistemology`; a concise philosophy source on induction and uniformity of nature
- **Public-response use:** ask how a purely impersonal worldview grounds logic, induction, and reason without circularity
