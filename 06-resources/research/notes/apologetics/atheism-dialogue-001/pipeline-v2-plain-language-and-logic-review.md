# Pipeline V2 Plain Language And Logic Review

## Hard Words Simplified Or Explained

- `category error` is now used with a plain explanation: treating two different kinds of questions as if they were the same.
- `metaphysical naturalism` is framed as the claim that only impersonal matter exists, not as a jargon term.
- `methodological naturalism` is explained as science as a method rather than a worldview.
- `ontology` is explained as what something is in reality.
- `induction` is explained as moving from observed regularity to expecting future regularity.
- `normativity` is explained through the question of why logic should be binding.
- `intelligibility` is explained as the assumption that nature can be understood and is not random chaos.
