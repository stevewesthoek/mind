## Claims That Need Proof Discipline

- Do not claim science proves materialism.
- Do not claim science proves atheism.
- Do not claim consciousness is solved by brain correlation.
- Do not claim fine-tuning proves design.
- Do not claim resurrection is historically uncontested.
- Do not claim bare atheism is identical to scientism.
- Do not retreat from the functional religion claim into a narrow legal-only definition.

## How To Argue The Functional Religion Claim

Use the following line of argument:

1. Atheism makes ultimate claims about reality, authority, and what exists.
2. Atheism functions as a worldview because it answers, or pretends to answer, questions of origin, meaning, morality, and authority.
3. Denying God does not leave people religiously neutral; it redirects allegiance toward man, science, naturalism, materialism, autonomy, or self.
4. The issue is not whether atheism is always a formal church-like institution.
5. The issue is whether atheism functions as religious allegiance in the functional/worldview sense.
6. The opponent must show why this functional definition is invalid, not merely insist on a narrower institutional definition.
