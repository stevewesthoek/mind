# Scientific Source Map

## Core Scientific Sources

### Origin Of Life

- `The origin of life: what we know, what we can know and what we will never know` - PMC
- Source link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3718341/
- Supports:
  - origin-of-life research remains one of the hardest scientific questions
  - systems chemistry advances do not equal a full solution
  - unresolved is not the same as solved
- Reliability note: strong, peer-reviewed review-style source
- Caution:
  - do not claim the paper proves design
  - do not claim the paper proves naturalism

### Consciousness

- `Facing up to the hard question of consciousness` - PMC
- Source link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6074080/
- Supports:
  - the hard problem remains a live issue
  - there is ongoing debate over what needs explaining
- Reliability note: useful for showing the problem remains active
- Caution:
  - this article argues one side of the debate; it does not settle it

- `Stable Consciousness? The “Hard Problem” Historically Reconstructed...` - PMC
- Source link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9185404/
- Supports:
  - the hard problem remains a recognized issue in neuroscience/philosophy discussion
  - researchers disagree on the explanandum and explanans
- Reliability note: good for showing ongoing disagreement
- Caution:
  - do not overstate consensus

### Methodological / Philosophical Naturalism

- `Naturalism` - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy
- Source link: https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2021/entries/naturalism/
- Supports:
  - methodological naturalism differs from philosophical naturalism
  - naturalism is a philosophical position, not just a scientific technique
- Reliability note: high
- Caution:
  - terminology varies across writers

- `Naturalism in Epistemology` - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy
- Source link: https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2024/entries/epistemology-naturalized/
- Supports:
  - epistemology is closely tied to science in some naturalistic approaches
  - this is a philosophical position, not an experimental result
- Reliability note: high
- Caution:
  - do not treat it as proof that naturalism is true

### Fine-Tuning

- `Fine-Tuning` - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy
- Source link: https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2025/entries/fine-tuning/
- Supports:
  - fine-tuning is a real philosophical issue
  - it is an inference problem, not a proof
- Reliability note: high
- Caution:
  - do not overstate the strength of the inference

## Scientific Claims That Stay Strong

- Evolution is not abiogenesis.
- Transitional fossils are abundant.
- The second law of thermodynamics does not rule out local complexity on Earth.
- Consciousness is strongly correlated with brain states.
- Science is self-correcting.
- Origin-of-life research remains incomplete.

## Scientific Claims That Need Care

- The universe began to exist.
- Fine-tuning supports design.
- DNA contains information in a meaningful sense.
- Natural selection is not a guarantee of truth-tracking cognition.
- Consciousness remains unexplained in a complete sense.

## Scientific Claims To Avoid

- Science proves materialism.
- Science proves atheism.
- Science explains morality fully.
- Science explains meaning fully.
- Fine-tuning proves design.
- The origin of life is solved.
