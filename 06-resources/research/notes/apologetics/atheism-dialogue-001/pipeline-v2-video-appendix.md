### 1. Peter Harrison - Natural Causes, Divine Action, and Scientific Explanation
- **Speaker/source:** Peter Harrison / Ian Ramsey Centre (Oxford)
- **URL:** https://www.ianramseycentre.ox.ac.uk/article/harrison-natural-causes-divine-action-and-scientific-explanation
- **Claim area:** science and metaphysics; methodological vs metaphysical naturalism
- **Metadata/transcript status:** Strong metadata; automatic captions available via embedded YouTube; no manual subtitles detected
- **Why useful:** It directly supports the distinction between science as method and naturalism as a worldview.
