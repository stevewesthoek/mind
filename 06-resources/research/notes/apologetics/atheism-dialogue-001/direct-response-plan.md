# Direct Response Plan

## Purpose

Turn the respondent’s actual objections into a direct, claim-by-claim rebuttal without collapsing into a soft worldview essay.

## Structure

1. Open with a short acknowledgment that some corrections are fair.
2. State clearly that the response will address the respondent’s claims point by point.
3. Group claims by category:
   - definitions
   - science and epistemology
   - consciousness and morality
   - origins and evolution
   - history and resurrection
   - rhetorical attacks and misrepresentations
4. For each claim:
   - quote or summarize it briefly
   - answer directly
   - name the error if present
   - concede only what is actually wrong on Steve’s side
5. End with a concise conclusion that restates the strongest remaining theistic case.

## Rebuttal Priorities

### Highest priority

- Atheism as lack of belief vs. naturalism as worldview
- Faith vs warranted trust
- Science as method vs scientism as philosophy
- The accusation that Steve is committing fallacies where he is actually making an epistemic or philosophical argument
- The respondent’s strongest misrepresentation claims

### Secondary priority

- Consciousness and the brain
- Moral realism vs reductionistic moral explanations
- Abiogenesis versus evolution
- Fine-tuning as inference, not proof
- Historical resurrection as cumulative argument

### Tone controls

- Be respectful, but do not flatten disagreement.
- Use direct phrases such as:
  - "That is not what I argued."
  - "This misrepresents my position."
  - "That conclusion does not follow."
  - "This is an assertion, not an argument."
  - "I can revise this wording without accepting your conclusion."
- Avoid softeners that weaken the rebuttal.

## Source Use

- Use Bible/theology only where posture or general-revelation framing is actually relevant.
- Use philosophy notes for atheism, naturalism, scientism, faith, logic, math, and moral realism.
- Use science notes only for brain/consciousness correlation, abiogenesis, evolution, entropy, and information claims.
- Use historical notes only for resurrection claims and historical method.

## Deliverable Shape

The eventual sendable response should:

- remain direct and claim-by-claim
- preserve concessions where Steve was too broad
- call out misrepresentation plainly
- keep the reply respectful without becoming generic
- avoid adding new arguments that are not already in the source set
