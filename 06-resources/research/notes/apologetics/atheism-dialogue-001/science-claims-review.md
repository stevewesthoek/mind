# Science Claims Review

## Scope

This review separates what science can support from what the dialogue overstates.

## Supported Claims

- Evolution is not abiogenesis.
- Transitional fossils exist in abundance.
- The second law of thermodynamics does not forbid local complexity on Earth.
- Consciousness is strongly correlated with brain states.
- Science is self-correcting.
- Origin-of-life research has not yet produced a full natural pathway from non-life to life in the lab.

## Plausible But Unsourced Claims

- The universe began to exist.
- Fine-tuning is real in the sense that constants appear life-permitting.
- DNA contains information in a meaningful sense.
- Natural selection is not a guarantee of truth-tracking cognition.
- Consciousness remains unexplained in a complete sense.

## Disputed Or Weak Claims

- Science proves materialism.
- Science proves atheism.
- Science explains morality fully.
- Science explains meaning fully.
- DNA information therefore proves intelligence.
- Fine-tuning therefore proves design.

## Claims To Remove

- Transitional forms are largely absent.
- Entropy makes evolution impossible.
- Breeding only destroys information.
- Science tells us the universe came from nothing.
- Life arose spontaneously as a matter of demonstrated fact.

## Needed Science Sources

- Paleontology for transitional fossils and branching evolution.
- Thermodynamics for entropy and open systems.
- Origin-of-life chemistry for abiogenesis limits and models.
- Neuroscience for brain/consciousness correlation.
- Cosmology for universe-beginning claims.
- Information theory for any DNA/information argument.

## Scientific Framing Advice

- Use "not yet fully explained" instead of "impossible."
- Use "consistent with" instead of "proves" unless the data are decisive.
- Do not use science as a substitute for philosophy.
