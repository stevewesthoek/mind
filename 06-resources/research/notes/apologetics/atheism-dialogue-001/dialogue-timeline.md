# Dialogue Timeline

## Scope

This timeline separates the three dialogue documents and records the main claim flow without rewriting the source texts.

## 01-original-paper.md

### Opening thesis

- Defines atheism as belief that God does not exist.
- Defines faith as trust or belief in what cannot be empirically proven.
- Frames atheism, scientism, evolution, and naturalism as one worldview package.

### Main argument ladder

- Metaphysics: reality is not purely material.
- Epistemology: evolved cognition may not be truth-tracking.
- Origins: life did not arise from non-life by demonstrated mechanism.
- Biology: evolution explains diversification, not the deeper origin questions.

### Positive case

- Cosmological argument.
- Fine-tuning argument.
- Origin-of-life argument.
- DNA/information argument.
- Consciousness argument.
- Moral argument.
- Rationality argument.
- Mathematical/logic argument.
- Historical Jesus / resurrection argument.

### Additional objections

- Scientism is self-refuting.
- Science cannot answer ultimate meaning questions.
- Evolution does not explain abiogenesis.
- Transitional forms, entropy, and breeding claims are used as objections to evolution.

## 02-atheist-response.md

### Opening correction

- Atheism is usually a lack of belief, not a positive claim that no god exists.
- The burden of proof is on the person making the god claim.

### Core rebuttal strategy

- Faith and trust are distinguished.
- Science is framed as self-correcting observation, not faith.
- Naturalism is treated as an evidence-based conclusion, not a starting assumption.
- Steve's framework is described as overgeneralizing atheism into scientism and naturalism.

### Main rebuttal themes

- Definitions are narrower than Steve's definitions.
- Science is bottom-up, not dependent on metaphysical first principles in the way Steve presents.
- Evolution is not the foundation of atheism.
- Math and logic are described as human descriptive tools rather than immaterial entities.
- Consciousness is linked to brain states.
- Morality is linked to biology and social cooperation.
- Abiogenesis is separated from evolution.
- Transitional fossils and entropy objections are rejected.
- Fine-tuning and resurrection claims are treated as overstated.

## 03-steve-reply.md

### Reply posture

- More direct and conversational.
- Reasserts Steve's core concern that atheism often functions as a worldview.
- Repeats several earlier claims with lighter tone but similar argument structure.

### Main continuities

- Maintains the same definition disputes.
- Reuses the same cosmological, moral, consciousness, and historical lines.
- Continues to treat atheism as philosophically loaded rather than neutral.

### Main pressure points

- Needs tighter definitions.
- Needs clearer separation of metaphysical claims from scientific claims.
- Needs stronger sourcing for historical and scientific assertions.
- Needs a calmer and more precise tone before a sendable response.
