# ProChat Customer Profiles

**Status:** canonical customer profiles  
**Owner:** Steve Westhoek  
**Last updated:** 2026-06-05

## Primary buyer profile

The best first buyer is a business owner, founder, manager, or team lead who sees repeated information work slowing the team down.

They have:

- repeated emails, notes, reports, proposals, or support replies
- scattered examples and business knowledge
- a clear sense that AI should help more than it currently does
- limited time to configure complex tools
- willingness to pay for a practical time-saving result

## Best first wedges

### Founder/owner

Pain:

- keeps explaining the same context
- decisions live in their head
- delegation takes too long
- proposals and replies are rewritten manually

Desired outcome:

```text
Drafts, summaries, instructions, and proposals that already fit how the business works.
```

### Sales

Pain:

- follow-ups take time
- proposals repeat patterns
- objections repeat
- the best examples are not reused

Desired outcome:

```text
Faster follow-ups, proposal drafts, objection responses, CRM notes, and next-step checklists.
```

### Support

Pain:

- customers ask similar questions
- answers need to be consistent
- knowledge is scattered
- escalations need clear summaries

Desired outcome:

```text
Faster, more consistent support replies and clearer escalation notes.
```

### Marketing/content

Pain:

- AI drafts do not match the business voice
- examples are scattered
- campaign work repeats
- notes do not become content quickly

Desired outcome:

```text
Drafts, outlines, briefs, and posts that are closer to the company's voice and examples.
```

### Operations/reporting

Pain:

- notes need to become tasks
- status updates repeat
- handoffs are unclear
- missing information slows people down

Desired outcome:

```text
Clearer reports, task lists, handoffs, and recurring updates.
```

## Qualification criteria

A good early prospect has:

- one repeated task they can name
- examples of past work
- a clear desired output
- a reviewer who can judge quality
- willingness to start small
- enough value in time saved to justify payment

## Poor early prospects

Avoid prospects who:

- want a broad AI transformation immediately
- cannot name a repeated task
- have no examples
- want full automation without review
- need heavy regulated-domain expertise before any value is visible
- expect a generic chatbot instead of a managed outcome

## First sale target

The first sale should make one person or one team faster.

Expansion comes later:

```text
one person → one team → one department → company memory
```
