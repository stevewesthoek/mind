## Purpose

This document defines the highest-level beliefs that govern ProChat as a company. Product, design, marketing, operational, and commercial decisions should be evaluated against these principles.

## Company belief

Useful work should leave useful knowledge behind.

AI can make individual tasks faster, but lasting value appears only when the understanding created during that work remains available, reviewable, and reusable.

ProChat exists to help people preserve that understanding and put it to work safely.

## Vision

A future in which people and teams do not repeatedly lose the decisions, evidence, corrections, and lessons created during real project work.

Project knowledge should remain understandable to people, usable through different AI interfaces, and controlled by the people responsible for the work.

## Mission

Build local, memory-first products that:

- preserve reviewed project knowledge;
- keep evidence and provenance visible;
- give people control over trust, scope, correction, and retirement;
- connect familiar AI interfaces to real project work;
- reduce repeated investigation and context reconstruction;
- remain honest about limitations, risk, and product maturity.

## Core philosophy

```text
Memory is the foundation.
Evidence keeps it trustworthy.
Human review improves it.
AI puts it to work.
```

### Memory is the foundation

The model is not the durable asset.

The durable asset is the reviewed knowledge produced through work:

- decisions;
- evidence;
- failures;
- fixes;
- corrections;
- procedures;
- examples;
- constraints;
- context;
- lessons learned.

### Evidence keeps it trustworthy

Memory without evidence can become confident repetition of an old or incorrect interpretation.

Important records should remain connected to their source, scope, review state, and last-known applicability where practical.

Current evidence and human judgment remain stronger than stored memory.

### Human review improves it

AI may help identify, structure, retrieve, compare, or apply knowledge.

AI does not silently decide what becomes trusted organisational memory or what sensitive operation should occur.

Trust is earned through explicit review, scope, correction, and accountability.

### AI puts it to work

Memory should not become an archive that people never use.

Relevant reviewed knowledge should improve later reasoning, investigation, writing, building, and decision-making through familiar AI interfaces.

## Company principles

### Local ownership

Customer memory and project context should remain under customer control.

ProChat should avoid creating unnecessary dependency on a hosted proprietary memory platform.

### Readable foundations

Durable knowledge should remain readable and inspectable by people.

Where practical, use open, understandable formats such as Markdown and Git-compatible history.

### Explicit trust

Draft, reviewed, approved, rejected, superseded, and retired states must remain distinguishable.

Unreviewed input must not silently become trusted knowledge.

### Evidence before certainty

Current evidence can invalidate earlier memory.

The product should support correction rather than defend prior output.

### Bounded assistance

AI-assisted actions should have clear scope, visible context, and explicit operational boundaries.

Automation is valuable only when its authority, evidence, and failure behavior are understandable.

### Honest product boundaries

ProChat should not market future capabilities as current products, imply guarantees that have not been measured, or hide beta-stage limitations.

### Simplicity through structure

Simple does not mean vague or empty.

ProChat reduces complexity through explicit relationships, state, scope, hierarchy, and progressive disclosure.

### Quality before breadth

ProChat should establish repeated value in one real discipline before expanding broadly.

A smaller reliable system is more valuable than a broad set of weak capabilities.

### Durable company building

Brand, product, design, documentation, and code should form one coherent system that can support future contributors, products, and customers.

Temporary speed must not create permanent confusion.

## Design philosophy

The public and product experience should feel like a calm, trustworthy working system for structured knowledge.

Premium quality comes from:

- precision;
- hierarchy;
- restraint;
- explanatory visuals;
- excellent typography;
- purposeful motion;
- accessibility;
- performance;
- realistic states;
- careful implementation.

It does not come from spectacle, visual noise, generic AI imagery, or ornamental complexity.

Operational design rule:

```text
Structure first.
Evidence visible.
Actions explicit.
Decoration restrained.
```

## Communication philosophy

ProChat communicates as a real software company.

The company voice should be:

- calm;
- direct;
- specific;
- practical;
- evidence-aware;
- technically credible;
- honest about current status.

Avoid:

- hype;
- guru language;
- vague transformation claims;
- fear-based selling;
- unsupported savings;
- absolute security or accuracy claims;
- architecture-first explanations before user value.

## Decision test

Before approving a product, design, copy, or commercial decision, ask:

1. Does it preserve or improve useful project knowledge?
2. Is the evidence and trust boundary understandable?
3. Does it keep people appropriately in control?
4. Is the claim true at the current product stage?
5. Does it create durable clarity rather than temporary novelty?
6. Can the result remain accessible, fast, and maintainable?
7. Does it strengthen the two-product company architecture?

If the answer is unclear, the decision is not ready.
