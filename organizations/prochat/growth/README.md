Growth activity should prioritize learning, qualified adoption, useful demonstrations, trusted relationships, and evidence of repeated value before monetization.

## Operating rules

All growth work must:

- select exactly one product lane for each asset or activity;
- use real product capabilities and real observations;
- remain honest about the pre-revenue beta stage;
- keep QA beta recruitment selected rather than mass-market;
- keep customer and client memory local;
- distinguish ProChat Memory for QA from ProChat Workbench;
- require human review before publishing;
- keep posting and outreach manual;
- avoid invented testimonials, evidence, metrics, or customer claims;
- avoid unsupported impression, follower, accuracy, cost, or performance claims;
- avoid presenting future capabilities as current products;
- avoid unrelated market positioning that does not support either product lane.
