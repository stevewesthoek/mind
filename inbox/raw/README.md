# Inbox — Raw

Immutable originals and source dumps live here after migration.

Use this for raw audio, original transcripts, pasted originals, screenshots, uploaded files, OCR outputs, and other evidence-grade intake artifacts.

Brain may summarize or propose links from raw material, but raw originals should not be rewritten as durable truth.

**Compatibility note:** legacy raw/original capture material remains in `capture/` until the controlled move to `inbox/raw/` or `resources/`. No move has occurred yet.
